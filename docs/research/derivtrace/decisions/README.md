# DerivTrace experimental revision register

This directory is reserved for a future register of experimental model
revisions. The register will preserve why a candidate changed without turning a
research revision into a normative product decision.

Each future revision record must preserve:

- the observed problem;
- the counterexample or observation;
- the affected model version;
- the proposed modification;
- the justification for that proposal;
- consequences for existing experiments;
- newly required verification.

A record must also preserve enough provenance to distinguish historical
observation, analytical reconstruction, hypothesis, and verified conclusion.
Its existence does not establish the proposal's validity or promote it into
Proto-Scope authority.

No revision or decision is recorded by this initial index.
