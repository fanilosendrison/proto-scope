## Evidence and execution classes

Assign every source and experimental result to a precise class:

- `IMMUTABLE_SOURCE_BYTES` — exact commit/blob and source-text integrity; no inherent truth of described events.
- `MUTABLE_ACQUIRED_RECORD` — issue/comment/API record, acquisition time/content fingerprint, and history limitations.
- `PRODUCER_DECLARATION` — reported action, outcome or qualification; independent execution unproven.
- `REPORTED_PRIOR_EXECUTION` — recorded command and output, still subject to execution-custody limitations.
- `PRESENT_CONTROLLED_REPLAY` — actual newly executed command in isolated fixtures with revision, environment, input, stdout/stderr, exit status, and owned effects.
- `CONSTRUCTED_COUNTEREXAMPLE` — bounded stipulated worlds; not a historical occurrence.
- `INDEPENDENT_QUALIFICATION` — independent analysis of an exact claim and evidence, not automatic authentication of all sources.

A recorded `PASS` or CI result must not be called an independently rerun test. Prefer genuine controlled replay where source and environment are available. Log `UNSUPPORTED_ENVIRONMENT` or `UNREPRODUCIBLE_WITH_AVAILABLE_EVIDENCE` rather than simulate execution and call it reproduced.

The v45 source/recorded results may be examined within their exact historical boundary.
