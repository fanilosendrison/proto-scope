## Problem

TURNLOCK requires normal completion of a workflow-declared nested invocation to restore the exact immediate caller context and make its preserved post-call continuation eligible under the enclosing workflow topology.

The existing assurance claim TL-CLAIM-011 describes correct return and authorization of the appropriate continuation.

A bounded SAT experiment found that a plausible literal formalization of the claim permits correct return and authorization without establishing positive post-call eligibility.

This is a candidate semantic-correspondence weakness, not a confirmed defect in the entire formal-assurance corpus.

## Current authoritative boundary

Accepted TURNLOCK normative semantics already explicitly distinguish:

- workflow-declared invocation return;
- agent-selected nested invocation return;
- post-call continuation eligibility;
- authorization of a continuation;
- independent concurrent sibling progression;
- conditional occurrence-scoped liveness.

The formal-assurance manifest is not permitted to weaken or redefine these semantics.

A product change is not authorized by this Issue.
