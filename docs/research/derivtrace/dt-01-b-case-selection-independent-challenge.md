## 15. Genuinely attempted new case-selection counterexample

### IC-RETRY-MULTIPLICITY — Coalesced success conceals duplicate committed effects

This case is constructed. It is not a historical incident.

**W**

```text
W = {w1, w2}
```

Both worlds concern one logical operation `L`, one retry/idempotency token `k`, one non-idempotent or multiplicity-sensitive external effect `E`, and a local contract requiring at most one committed `E` before continuation.

**w1 — one committed effect**

- attempt `e1` commits `E` once;
- its acknowledgement is lost;
- a retry occurs;
- the retry is deduplicated before committing another `E`;
- the reporting layer exposes one successful logical operation and final local artifact `F`.

**w2 — duplicate committed effect**

- attempt `e1` commits `E`;
- deduplication state is stale, partitioned, or unavailable;
- retry attempt `e2` also commits `E`;
- the reporting layer coalesces both attempts under token `k`;
- it exposes the same single success record and the same local artifact `F`.

**Exact Q**

```text
Q(w) =
TRUE iff logical operation L committed at most one materially relevant
effect E before the examined continuation.
```

**Exact O**

```text
O(w) = {
  logical operation L,
  token k,
  one coalesced success/PASS record,
  final local artifact F
}
```

O excludes:

- attempt identities and multiplicity;
- commit identities;
- the external effect ledger;
- deduplication-store state;
- atomicity guarantees;
- retry causality; and
- any qualified external audit.

**Equality and unequal truth values**

```text
O(w1) = O(w2)

Q(w1) = TRUE
Q(w2) = FALSE
```

**Constructed assumptions**

- `E` is non-idempotent or its multiplicity is material.
- The reporting layer coalesces by `k`.
- `F` does not expose the number of external commits.
- The local contract requires at-most-once effect semantics before continuation.
- No compensation has already restored the required property in w2.

**External evidence versus construction**

External packet evidence supports the materiality of occurrence multiplicity:

- Turnlock TL-INV-033 requires occurrence distinctions and multiplicity to remain inspectable when their collapse would lose realized progression.
- TL-CLAIM-052 similarly identifies multiplicity as material when needed to determine progression.
- #23’s hypothetical capture boundaries mention retry and duplication semantics.

Those sources do not establish w2 historically. The pair itself is an analytical construction.

**Potentially distinguishing evidence**

Potential evidence includes:

- authenticated attempt identities;
- complete commit/effect records;
- idempotency-store state and revision;
- an atomic deduplication-and-commit guarantee;
- exact retry causality;
- external-system audit bound to `L`, `k`, subject, and revision; and
- compensation evidence if the contract permits repair.

No such evidence is established as effectively available by #21–#23.

**Why existing targets do not already cover it**

- K3 asks whether a STOP event occurred, not how many effectful executions committed.
- K5 asks whether X occurred and whether a declaration is truthful, not whether one logical operation produced multiple commits.
- Known retry/duplication semantics in hypothetical capture boundaries are assumptions about capture behavior, not a target examining effect multiplicity.
- K6 concerns authority for one exact atomic event, not retry-induced multiple effects.

**Target classifications**

- `IC-RETRY-MULTIPLICITY`: `EXAMINED`. The construction establishes that O is insufficient for Q.
- `IC-RETRY-ACTUAL`: `NOT EXAMINED`. No historical duplicate-effect case is selected.

**Continuation consequence**

- In w1, ordinary continuation may be warranted under the stipulated contract.
- In w2, continuation may require stopping, reconciliation, compensation, or preservation of an unresolved violation.

The exact response depends on the local contract; the construction establishes only that the same O cannot justify the same answer in both worlds.
